# Research notes: world news of Friday 20 November 2026

Date convention: "20 Nov" = news reported on Friday 20 Nov 2026 (UTC). Some events happened late on
Thu 19 Nov and were reported on the 20th; the image says so in its footer and tags them where it matters.
Research was done late on 20 Nov 2026 (UTC) with web search plus the daily briefings below, so events
after that hour are not covered.

## Caveats and choices

- The image does not give a bridge count for Kyiv: Kyiv Independent reported two bridges hit on 16-17 Nov,
  Democracy Now! says three in its 20 Nov headlines.
- The Estonian "at least six months" detail comes from one source (Just Security); other outlets only report
  the closure from 1 Dec without a duration.
- Considered and dropped for lack of a second source: a Nigerian boat disaster (Bayelsa, ~30 dead, ~300 missing).
- Also in the 20 Nov news but not used: Somalia ordering the UAE ambassador out in 72 hours; Save the Children
  report that 1 in 4 children live in conflict zones; GTA VI release; South Korea's Supreme Court overturning
  Han Duck-soo's sentence; Gaza strikes.
- One search tool summary claimed a Trump-Xi-Putin "joint declaration"; it came from an aggregator, so it was
  only used after Anadolu confirmed it.
